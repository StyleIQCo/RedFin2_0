How the strategy engine works

AVM-anchored pricing with market-aware adjustments.
1. AVM Anchor
LightGBM predicts the objective fair value. Both buyer and seller prices are derived as percentages of this anchor — not of the asking price, which is biased.
2. Market condition
City median price from 50k listings determines baseline temperature: Hot (>$1.4M) → seller leverage, Cool (<$500k) → buyer leverage. Buyer bids range from +3% (hot) to −10% (cold) of AVM.
3. Dynamic adjustments
Days-on-market, competing offers, year built, school score, and walk score each shift the multipliers in real time. A 60-day listing shifts buyer leverage by −4%; 3+ competing offers shift seller leverage by +2.5%.
4. Tactic narrative
Claude generates buyer/seller advice when ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set; a rule-based engine provides market-specific tactics offline.
